# Elevatelabs-DA-Task8
Objective: Create a basic interactive dashboard that shows sales performance by product, region, and month.
![Screenshot 2025-05-03 024942](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8bb45ed3-e7e0-41ef-ad9d-6b2d6b7e93e1)
## Total Sales: 
Represents the overall revenue generated across all outlets, allowing analysis of peak and off-peak sales periods.
## Average Sales: 
Measures the average sales value, helping identify performance benchmarks and seasonal trends.
## Number of Items: 
Tracks the quantity of products, essential for inventory planning and demand forecasting.
## Average Rating: 
Displays the average customer rating per product or outlet, providing insights into customer satisfaction and areas for improvement.
## Outlet Size: 
Analyzes sales performance based on outlet size (e.g., small, medium, large) to assess size-based efficiency.
## Outlet Type: 
Differentiates sales across outlet types (e.g., Breads, Dairy) to identify the most profitable types.
## Outlet Establishment: 
Examines the impact of the outlet's age on sales, helping evaluate newer vs. established locations.
Fat Content: Tracks product-specific data on fat content (e.g., low-fat, regular) to understand customer preferences and health trends.
